The Journey

Building a Global Platform for the Nature-Based Economy

Since its launch in 2025, the Nature Summit Series has evolved into a growing global platform that convenes leaders at the intersection of climate, biodiversity, land, oceans, finance, technology, and sustainable development — unlocking the value of nature for people, planet, and prosperity.

  1. 2025 · UNFCCC

    Nature Summit for Climate

    Panama City · Alongside UNFCCC Global Climate Week

    The inaugural Nature Summit convened leaders from government, finance, philanthropy, business, and civil society to explore how the nature-based economy can accelerate climate resilience, sustainable development, and economic opportunity.

  2. 2025 · CBD

    Nature Summit for Biodiversity

    Alongside the Convention on Biological Diversity

    Building on the momentum of the inaugural summit, leaders gathered to explore how biodiversity can be recognized, valued, and integrated into strategies for investment, conservation, and long-term prosperity.

  3. 2025 · UNCCD

    Nature Summit for Land

    Alongside the UN Convention to Combat Desertification

    The third chapter focused on land restoration, food security, resilience, and sustainable development — bringing together leaders committed to advancing practical solutions for communities and ecosystems worldwide.

  4. 2026 · EarthX

    Nature Summit at EarthX

    Dallas, Texas · Earth Day

    The Nature Summit expanded beyond multilateral negotiations, engaging investors, innovators, government leaders, entrepreneurs, and philanthropists around emerging opportunities within the nature-based economy.

  5. 2026 · UN General Assembly

    Nature Summit at the UN

    United Nations Headquarters · New York City

    The next chapter.

    Convening leaders from across government, capital, industry, philanthropy, and multilateral institutions during the 81st UN General Assembly to strengthen relationships, align priorities, and accelerate partnerships capable of shaping the future of the nature-based economy.

The Opportunity

“Nature is the greatest economic opportunity of the 21st century.”

For generations, economic growth has largely been built upon the extraction of nature. The emerging nature-based economy offers a different path — one that recognizes the immense value generated by healthy oceans, thriving forests, productive landscapes, and resilient ecosystems.

These living systems regulate climate, support global commerce, strengthen food and water security, reduce disaster risk, and sustain billions of livelihoods around the world.

They are not simply environmental assets. They are economic assets.

A new generation of leaders is working to make nature visible within markets, policy frameworks, investment strategies, and development models.

By aligning capital, innovation, technology, and governance around the value of living systems, governments and investors have an unprecedented opportunity to unlock sustainable growth, strengthen resilience, and create prosperity — particularly for nations and communities on the frontlines of climate change, biodiversity loss, and environmental degradation.

The Nature Summit exists to help accelerate that transition.

Why the UN General Assembly

The World's Most Influential Gathering

Every year, the United Nations General Assembly convenes heads of state, ministers, ambassadors, investors, philanthropists, business leaders, and international institutions. The 2026 Nature Summit provides a unique platform within that moment for leaders focused on building the future of the nature-based economy.

As governments seek resilience, investors seek opportunity, businesses seek growth, and communities seek prosperity, the need for collaboration across sectors has never been greater. The Nature Summit creates a space for those conversations — and the partnerships that follow.
